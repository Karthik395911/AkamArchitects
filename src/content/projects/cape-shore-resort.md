description: >
  Located in Mangatukonam, Thiruvananthapuram, the Jithin Residence is a
  contemporary tropical home designed to balance modern architectural expression
  with the warmth and character of Kerala's traditional building materials.


  Set on a compact 5.46-cent plot, the 2,449 sq. ft. residence demonstrates an approach to residential design that prioritises spatial efficiency, natural light, ventilation, and a strong connection with its tropical surroundings.


  The architectural identity is defined by flowing roof profiles, exposed terracotta brickwork, intricately patterned brick jaali screens, and clean white surfaces. These elements create a distinctive façade while introducing texture, depth, and visual interest. The curved architectural forms soften the building's overall appearance, contrasting with the structured geometry of its openings and brick detailing.


  Inside, the residence features thoughtfully connected living spaces, warm wooden finishes, and carefully integrated natural lighting. The double-height spaces, strategically positioned windows, and decorative screens contribute to an open and airy atmosphere while maintaining privacy.


  The landscape complements the architecture through tropical planting, paved outdoor spaces, and carefully considered transitions between indoor and outdoor areas.


  Commenced in 2024 and completed in 2026, the Jithin Residence reflects AKAM Architects' commitment to creating functional, context-responsive homes that combine contemporary design with Kerala's architectural character.
